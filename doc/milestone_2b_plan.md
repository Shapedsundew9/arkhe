### 2.1 Resolution of the Redundancy Funding Paradox (Charter DR #40)

In the unamended M1/M2a codebase, placing an independent derivation $\hat{y}_{\text{alt}}$ plus `SUB` and `EQZ` onto an already-solved port $p$ deterministically fails for three independent reasons:

1. **Zero boundary counterfactual sensitivity (`D1b.1` excision):** Perturbing `alt`, `SUB`, or `EQZ` leaves `BoundaryOut(p)` unchanged, so `prune_zero_sensitivity_mature_nodes` ([`src/structural/surgery.rs`](../src/structural/surgery.rs)) removes the entire comparator subgraph at the first mature diagnostic check.
2. **Unfunded wake maturity debt:** Under single-stream boundary MDL, once primary derivation $\hat{y}_{\text{primary}}$ hits `0` mismatches, adding `alt + SUB + EQZ` increases $L(C)$ by $+40.1$ to $+137.3$ bits while saving `0` boundary data bits; under *"sleep-only"* comparator reward, every redundancy sprout hits graduation (`juvenile_ticks = 0`) in wake with $E = E_{\text{init}} - \Delta L_i < 0$ and starves before sleep begins.
3. **Wake hallucination & corruption-inversion trap:** Scoring `alt(t) == primary(t)` blindly in wake allows two phase-locked wrong oscillators to feed each other positive energy while contradicting observed facts, and **penalises** a clean backup derivation $\hat{y}_{\text{alt}}$ whenever $\hat{y}_{\text{primary}}$'s driver channel becomes corrupted—destroying the backup pathway right when channel-fault recovery needs it.

**Ratified Law (`M2B-D1` / Charter §3.1.4 & §3.4, DR #40):**

- Each registered `ComparatorSite` $m$ (`sub_node = SUB(primary, alt)`, `eqz_node = EQZ(sub_node)` on `target_port = p`) owns a `PointForecastScorer` sharing/tracking port $p$'s wake null model $P_{\text{null}}^{(p)}$.
- **Wake (`ExecutionPhase::Wake`):** Scores `alt(t)` prequentially against observed ground truth `truth[p]` under $P_{\text{null}}^{(p)}$; routes payout exclusively through comparator $m$'s causal ancestry (`alt` cone + `sub_node` + `eqz_node`, stopping at `sub_node.in(0)` so pre-funded `primary` nodes never siphon comparator credit). Constant predictors and silent streams score identically `0.0`, and mutually agreeing hallucinations incur negative mismatch loss against `truth[p]`.
- **Sleep / Offline Loopback (`ExecutionPhase::Sleep`):** Boundary port rewards are disabled (`0.0`). Comparator $m$ scores internal agreement `alt(t) == primary(t)` (`eqz_node == 1`) against **frozen** wake $P_{\text{null}}^{(p)}$, weighting positive agreement by source-channel independence $\iota(a, b) = \frac{1}{\lvert C_a \cap C_b \rvert + 1}$ (`1.0` when $C_a \cap C_b = \emptyset$ or autonomous-vs-reactive; `0.5` when sharing 1 boundary channel) and weighting disagreement (`eqz_node == 0`) by full `1.0` under the Asymmetry Principle (Charter §1.2, §1.3).
- **Exact Epistemic MDL Conservation (Charter §3.4):** With `juvenile_ticks = 0` and `ReserveCap::Uncapped`, total energy change across all nodes identically equals minus null-calibrated epistemic MDL change:
  $$\Delta E(T) = F_{\text{boundary}}(T) + F_{\text{comparators}}(T) - L(C) \equiv -\Delta L_{\text{null}}^{\text{epistemic}}(T),$$
  reducing bit-identically to $-\Delta L_{\text{null}}(T)$ whenever $M = 0$.

2. **Per-node causal-dilution ceiling (`Anti-Parasite Guard`):** $t_{\text{juv}}(V_{\text{new}}) \le H_{\text{node}}(V_{\text{new}}) = \lceil 2 W_{\text{obs}} \cdot \max(t_{\text{base}} / t_{\text{juv,min}},\, Z_{\text{tot}}) \rceil$, where $Z_{\text{tot}} \ge 1.0$ is mean active sink ancestry (`port_shares[p].sum()` in Stage 2; `Z_alt + 2` in Stage 3).

### 2.3 Five Structural Invariants Governing `M2b` Implementation

To prevent regressions across `verify-m1`, `verify-m2a`, `tests/golden_traces.rs`, and `tests/regime_shift.rs`, the implementation **must** enforce these five invariants:

1. **Strict Stage Ordering (`Stage 1 -> Stage 2 -> Stage 3`):** On each mature, primed port check in [`ArkheLearner::observe`](../src/structural/learner.rs):
   - *Stage 1 (`D1b.1` idle pruning, comparator-aware):* Excises mature nodes with zero sensitivity over `W_diag` to **both** `BoundaryOut` and all registered `ComparatorSite::eqz_node` outputs.
   - *Stage 2 (Boundary corrector + autonomous ring + lag deepening):* Runs unchanged whenever `!is_port_zero_error(p)`.
   - *Stage 3 (Redundancy sprouting):* Runs **strictly after Stage 2 returns `None`**, only when `growth_config.redundancy_sprouting` is `true`, `BoundaryOut(p)` is driven, port $p$ (plus any existing comparator cone on $p$) is mature and primed, `scorer[p].q_hat() > p_null`, and `active_derivations(p) == 1` (or `== 2` with an existing comparator in persistent unresolved disagreement). Redundancy sprouts increment `redundancy_sprout_events` (never `port_sprout_events`), keeping `G1B.1`'s 3-epoch boundary snapshots (`0 -> 1 -> 2`) untouched.
2. **All-Port Non-Regression Guard (`TicksPerHop` Phase Safety) + Buffered Graduation Survival Check:**
   - In `WireDelayMode::TicksPerHop`, inserting a new `Primitive::Split` between a non-`Split` primary node `u` and `BoundaryOut(p)` adds `+1` pipeline hop (`+1` tick of phase shift) to `BoundaryOut(p)` unless `BoundaryOut(p)` is already driven via a `Split` with a free port or an autonomous ring whose register phase is shifted by `+1 mod P`.
   - Therefore `propose_redundancy_sprout` **must** enforce `e_cand_by_port[q] <= e_curr_by_port[q]` across **all** driven ports `q in 0..K` (**including `q == target_port`**) over the replay window, **must** reject lossy Stage 3 candidates (`e_alt_diag > 0`) whenever the primary port is noise-free (`e_curr_by_port[target_port] == 0`, since contradicting observed ground facts violates Charter §1.2 fallibilism while prefix-aliased `G2B.3` routes have `e_alt_diag == 0`), and **must** verify exact per-node graduation survival (`energy > 0.0` for all new nodes, mirroring `simulate_ring_juvenile_survival`) before committing.
3. **Strict Causal-Support Disjointness & Cross-Channel Priority (`M2B-D2` / DR #41):**
   - For port $p$, compute excluded interior computation/state nodes $\mathcal{V}_{\text{excl}}$ (all nodes in existing derivations' cones except pure fan-out `Split` trees directly on `BoundaryIn(c)`) and excluded elementary causal leaves $\mathcal{L}_{\text{excl}}$ (each reactive `(BoundaryIn(c), effective_lag)` path and `HasAutonomousCycle(p)`).
   - Mask $\mathcal{V}_{\text{excl}}$ and $\mathcal{L}_{\text{excl}}$ out of the Stage 3 candidate feature pool for target trace `diag_buf.truth[..][p]`, and allow an autonomous delay ring backup iff `!HasAutonomousCycle(p)`.
   - Partition passing candidates into **Tier 1** (channel-disjoint, $C_{\text{alt}} \cap C_{\text{primary}} = \emptyset$, including autonomous-vs-reactive) and **Tier 2** (shared-channel disjoint lag/state support, $C_{\text{alt}} \cap C_{\text{primary}} \ne \emptyset$ with $\mathcal{L}_{\text{alt}} \cap \mathcal{L}_{\text{excl}} = \emptyset$), selecting Tier 1 whenever an exact (`e_alt == 0`) or superior-`J` Tier 1 candidate exists.
4. **Dual-Timescale Log-Odds Driver Handoff & Two-Clause Wake Disagreement Pruning (`M2B-A1`, Zero Thrashing Under 5% Noise):**
   - Under stationary $\varepsilon = 0.05$ noise (e.g. `G1B.2b:NoisyAlternating` at `9.5%` reactive miss or `MV_AllChNoise` at `13.55%`–`14.3%` 2-leaf miss), `c(t) == 0` fires on `~5%–14%` of ticks while **both** derivations earn positive expected calibrated flux, yet ~1,600 correlated sliding `W_diag = 64` checks hit `17–19/64` miss tails (`-2.7` bits) and would falsely excise valid comparators (`4/20` to `10/20` runs).
   - **Step A — Reversible Driver Handoff (`driver_handoff_events`, horizon $W_{\text{diag}} = 64$):** Over a mature comparator's trailing `W_diag` window (`len >= W_diag`), if `e_alt < e_primary` and cumulative calibrated reward advantage $\sum_{t \in W_{\text{diag}}} [r_{\text{alt}}(t) - r_{\text{primary}}(t)] > \Delta L_{\text{comp}}$ (marginal code bits of `SUB + EQZ`), swap `alt_src` to drive `BoundaryOut(p)` and `sub_node.in(0)`, moving old `primary_src` to `sub_node.in(1)` ($\le 64$-tick fault recovery).
   - **Step B — Irreversible Wake Disagreement Pruning & 1-Sided Bankruptcy Refutation (`disagreement_prune_events`, `M2B-A1` & `M2B-B2`):** Excise a comparator-linked derivation $d$ (and its comparator) via `excise_port_comparator` iff **(i)** over the derived `observation_horizon` $W_{\text{obs}} = t_{\text{juv}} + 2 W_{\text{diag}} = 256$ (`len >= W_obs`), `disagreements(W_obs) >= min_match_gain` (`4`) and $\sum_{W_{\text{obs}}} r_d(t) \le 0 < \sum_{W_{\text{obs}}} r_{\text{peer}}(t)$, **OR (ii)** `starved_nodes` strikes a mature node of comparator $m$ (`sub_node`, `eqz_node`, or exclusive arm) while `len >= W_diag`, `disagreements(W_diag) >= min_match_gain`, $\sum_{\text{len}} r_{\text{worse}}(t) \le 0$, and the peer derivation has zero starved nodes with $\sum_{W_{\text{diag}}} r_{\text{peer}}(t) > 0$ (resolving the metabolic-bankruptcy race when severe post-fault losses bankrupt `sub_node` before `len` reaches `256`). At $W_{\text{obs}} = 256$, stationary `5%`-noise single-window tail is $3.88 \times 10^{-15}$–$1.57 \times 10^{-11}$ (`0/180` false prunes in `G2B.4`). When **both** derivations become obsolete simultaneously (`Alt(0,1) -> Constant(0)` in [`tests/regime_shift.rs`](../tests/regime_shift.rs)) or agree (`disagreements == 0` in `G2B.1a` controls), peer solvency / disagreement fails so starvation proceeds via `starvation_prune_events`.
5. **Cross-Port `TickBudget` Non-Starvation & Two-Pass Stage Scheduling (`M2B-A3`):**
   - Within [`ArkheLearner::observe`](../src/structural/learner.rs), evaluate **Stage 1 + Stage 2 across all eligible ports in Pass 1** before evaluating **Stage 3 redundancy sprouting in Pass 2**, deferring Stage 3 on port $p$ when any sibling port has juvenile nodes or has not yet completed at least one mature Stage-2 diagnostic pass (**never** blocking on `!is_port_driven(q)`, since unpredicted high-degree exogenous LFSR channels legitimately remain `0`-node / `!is_port_driven` for the entire horizon).
   - In `port_budget_allowance`, treat ports that have completed both Stage 2 (`is_port_zero_error(q)`) and Stage 3 (`!port_redundancy_arity_eligible(q)`) as idle donors to `idle_pool`, allowing ports requiring either Stage 2 or Stage 3 to draw unused surplus (while capping Stage 3 to `base_share` only while an unsolved sibling is actively progressing in Stage 2). On high-degree `deg 11 × deg 9` `G2A.5` tasks, once `(ch0, d_0)` and `(ch1, d_1)` are masked, zero remaining lags pass `mi_threshold_bits`, consuming `0` replay ticks.

## 5. Kill & Revisit Criteria

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Per Charter §7 and Rule 11, a kill or revisit outcome is a valid experimental result. If triggered on development or holdout runs, the agent records the finding, writes a blocker report ([`doc/templates/blocker_report.md`](templates/blocker_report.md)), and escalates rather than altering mechanisms, thresholds, or tasks to avoid it.

- **Kill / revisit condition (Charter §7 `M2b`):**
  1. Spontaneous `EQZ(SUB(primary, alt))` comparators fail to form or survive maturity across declared multi-view `T2` / multi-route `T1` tasks on development banks (pooled pass rate `< 0.50` on `G2B.2` after Phase 2, or two consecutive fix attempts produce no new diagnostic understanding);
  2. Default `redundancy_sprouting = true` degrades `M1` (`verify-m1`) or `M2a` (`verify-m2a` / `G2B.1(b)`) below pre-registered gates, or causes `effective_max_nodes` (`32` at $K=1$, `32 K` at $K > 1$) to bind (`sprout_blocked_max_nodes > 0`);
  3. Wake comparator disagreement fails to beat `redundancy_sprouting = false` under declared driver corruption (`G2B.5` pooled mean advantage `<= 0.0` b/sym).
  - **Action:** Stop immediately, file a blocker report per [`doc/templates/blocker_report.md`](templates/blocker_report.md), and escalate to the user.
