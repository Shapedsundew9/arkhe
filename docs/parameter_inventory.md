# Parameter & Constant Inventory

**Status:** Canonical inventory (Rule 2 of [`.github/copilot-instructions.md`](../.github/copilot-instructions.md), migrated from retired `doc/coordinate_free_scaling_plan.md` §2.1 under Readiness `C4`; see [research_record.md §1.2](research_record.md#12-archival-consolidation-of-superseded-milestone-1-pre-m2--milestone-2a-plans)).  
**Discipline:** Every tunable scalar, bound, threshold, numerical clamp, or statistical policy in `src/structural/` and `src/verify_*/` is classified here as **Derived**, **Milestone-Scoped Scaffolding**, **Pre-registered**, **Search bound**, **Compute guardrail**, or **Numerical guard**, with observable telemetry when any limit binds and concrete verification evidence. **Milestone-Scoped Scaffolding** constants are calibrated at an initial milestone's scale ($K=1, \tau \le 3$) and evolve into reasoned **Derived** scaling laws (no arbitrary magic numbers—every constant or scaling relationship must have a documented, reasoned basis) when a later milestone expands a scale axis ($\tau, K, k, N$).

### 2.1 Parameter Scaling Derivations & Deferred Rules

Durable mathematical derivations consolidated from retired `doc/coordinate_free_scaling_plan.md` §§2.2–2.8:

- **Lifecycle of Milestone-Scoped Scaffolding Constants ([`.github/copilot-instructions.md` Rule 2](../.github/copilot-instructions.md)):** Constants fixed to make an early milestone tractable (such as `juvenile_ticks = 128` or `diagnostic_window = 64` at $K=1, \tau \le 3$) are **Milestone-Scoped Scaffolding**, not immutable forever. When a subsequent milestone expands a scale axis ($\tau, K, k, N$) and a scaffolding constant binds for a reasoned structural or mathematical cause (e.g., $\gamma^{\tau-1}$ credit attenuation and $\tau$-tick pipeline fill across deep `Delay` chains), it is replaced by a reasoned **Derived** scaling law (no magic numbers; reducing identically to the M1 value at M1 scale where appropriate, or re-blessing `tests/golden_traces.rs` with written rationale and prior-milestone verification suite invariance proof).
- **Horizon-derived reserve ceiling (`ReserveCap::HorizonDerived`, DR #31 & M2a `3-a`):** Under `CalibratedPointForecast`, mismatch loss is $\log_2((1-q)/(1-p_{\text{null}}))$ (set by empirical predictor confidence $q$, not $O(k)$ width; width enters hit reward $\log_2(q/p_{\text{null}})$ via $p_{\text{null}} \sim 2^{-k}$). Earlier drafts proposing $k / \ln 2$ or debt multiplier $\beta = 2.0$ ("recovering `25.0`") were rejected as spurious unit conversions and post-hoc fitting. Option B sets $E_{\max}(v) = \max(E_{\text{init}}, W_{\text{obs}} \cdot \text{effective\_drain}(v))$ with no free multiplier, where instant $\text{drain}(v) = \sum_p \text{share}(v, p) \log_2((1-p_{\text{null}}(p))/(1-\hat{q}_p))$ (`1 - q` guarded by `RESERVE_MISMATCH_DENOM_FLOOR = 1e-12`) and across mature ticks `smoothed_drain_rate` persists $\text{effective\_drain} = \max(\text{drain}, \gamma \cdot \text{smoothed\_drain\_rate})$ so transient single-tick zero-share dropouts decay geometrically instead of collapsing $E_{\max}(v)$ to $E_{\text{init}}$. Because $\hat{q}_p$ falls as mismatches accumulate and partial matches still pay reward, $E_{\max}(v)$ does not guarantee starvation in $\le W_{\text{obs}}$ ticks universally (see [§3.2](#32-b6-multi-seed-regime-shift-latency-distribution)).
- **Compute guardrail (`max_nodes` & `effective_max_nodes`, DR #33, M2a `1-a`, & M3 `Option 4B` `#46`):** Scaling $N_{\max} = K \times 16$ was rejected at $K=1$ because it halved M1's limit (`32 -> 16`). At `M2a` (`redundancy_sprouting = false`), `effective_max_nodes(K, growth_config) = K * max(max_nodes, 2 * deepening_max_lag + 8)` (`32 K`) prevented deep lag-band binary pairings on one unsolved port from starving unrelated channels while preserving `32` at $K \le 1$. Under `M2b`/`M3` (`redundancy_sprouting = true`), each channel additionally maintains a Stage-3 causal-disjoint backup derivation (lag depth $\le \tau_{\text{deep}}$), `COMPARATOR_PRIMITIVE_COUNT = 2` (`Sub`, `Eqz`), and `2` fan-out `Split` taps, adding `stage3_allowance = tau_deep + COMPARATOR_PRIMITIVE_COUNT + 2` (`16` at defaults) to `floor = (2 * tau_deep + 8) + stage3_allowance` (`48 K`, i.e. `144` at $K=3$, while remaining `32` for $K \le 1$ and `32 K` when `!redundancy_sprouting`). Checked once per candidate prior to replay (`SproutMotifOutcome::BlockedMaxNodes`) and emitted at proposal level (`sprout_blocked_max_nodes += 1`) only when `best_circuit.is_none()` (and per-period in `evaluate_ring_period`); any run with `SproutBlockedMaxNodes > 0` marks the case `Excluded`.
- **Maturation-horizon MDL amortization (`J_mat` / `rank_scale`, M2a `2-a`) & `effective_juvenile_ticks` (`d2-A`):** Interior node credit attenuates by $\gamma^{d_{\text{cone}} - \tau_{\max}}$ along deep synchronous delay chains, so metabolic survival (`projected_survival`) and node maturation grace (`node.juvenile_ticks`) scale with `effective_juvenile_ticks(d_cone)` (`128` at $d_{\text{cone}} \le 3$). By contrast, output-port statistical data compression (`facts_code_bits` over `W_diag`) is depth-invariant: `amort_scale` and `rank_scale` both use the uniform horizon ratio `(juvenile_ticks / W_diag).max(1.0)` (`2.0` at defaults for $K > 1$ or $d_{\text{cone}} > \tau_{\max}$, `1.0` at $K=1, d_{\text{cone}} \le 3$), avoiding exponential $\gamma^{-d}$ overfitting of noise while evaluating $L(C)$ against the same maturation horizon across `W_diag = 64`.
- **Sibling-port non-regression (`4-a`):** Multi-channel `evaluate_sprout_motif` records per-port mismatches on all currently driven sibling ports `q != out_port` (`e_curr_by_port[q]`, `e_cand_by_port[q]`) and rejects any candidate that regresses a driven sibling (`e_cand_by_port[q] > e_curr_by_port[q]`), protecting locked cones against upstream `Split` hop shifts under `WireDelayMode::TicksPerHop`.
- **Per-port cone-local maturity (`PortDevelopmentalState`, DR #34):** Each output sink $p$ maintains its own `DiagnosticBuffer`, sprout cadence timer, primed check, and cone-local maturity predicate $\text{CanSprout}(p) \iff \forall v \in \text{UpstreamCone}(p),\ \text{juvenile}(v) = 0$. Juvenile node outputs are excluded from every port's candidate feature pool (`build_signal_pool`), structural edits reset only ports where `port_cone_changed` holds, and sprout checks rotate starting port via `tick mod K` (reducing identically to M1 at $K = 1$).
- **Unified observation horizon ($W_{\text{obs}}$, DR #31):** Both `ObservationWindow` capacity and `HorizonDerived` reserve bounding call `observation_horizon(&econ, &growth) = t_juv + 2 * W_diag` (`256` at defaults). Note $W_{\text{obs}} \ge W^* = \Delta L / \Delta I$ holds for M1 `NoisyAlternating(k=1)` ($W^* \approx 152.9$) but is not a universal theorem across arbitrary motif debts or noise levels; `CandidateUndecidable` counts candidates whose crossover exceeds `obs_window`.
- **Miller–Madow support & M5 estimator scaling:** `score_feature_group` uses observed empirical joint and residual support. However, empirical support does not prevent plug-in bias from exceeding entropy at large alphabets (e.g., at $N = 64$ with all-distinct symbols, $(63 \cdot 63)/(2 \cdot 64 \ln 2) \approx 44.7$ bits vs. $\log_2 64 = 6$ bits). M5 will evaluate (1) scaling $W_{\text{diag}}$ with observed joint support vs. (2) replacing (`mi_threshold_bits`, Miller–Madow, `min_match_gain`) with a single prequential KT code-length gain test.
- **Iterative lag & candidate search deepening (DR #32, implemented in M2a `e9fc185` / `918cb02`):** Reactive lag $\tau_{\max} = 3$, `candidate_feature_cap = 6`, and ring period range `2..=6` are search bounds rather than model constraints (since materialized `Delay` nodes pay exact $L(C)$). On multi-channel runs (`K > 1`), autonomous delay-ring closure (`Stage 2b`) runs before progressive delta bands `(3..=6] -> (6..=12]` up to `deepening_max_lag = 12` (`C2-a`), deferring lossy Stage-0 (`e_cand > 0`) commits across deepening so exact deep-lag couplings beat transient short-lag correlations while preserving exact M1 reduction at $K = 1$.
