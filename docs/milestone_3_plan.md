## 2. Exploratory Characterisation & Upfront Blocker Resolution Strategy

Per Rule 8 and [Chief Scientist Role §3 Principles 1–6](chief_scientist_role.md#3-seven-operating-principles), verbatim inspection of [`src/structural/surgery.rs`](../src/structural/surgery.rs), [`src/structural/sensitivity.rs`](../src/structural/sensitivity.rs), [`src/structural/economy.rs`](../src/structural/economy.rs), [`src/structural/growth.rs`](../src/structural/growth.rs), [`src/structural/graph_edit.rs`](../src/structural/graph_edit.rs), and [`src/structural/learner.rs`](../src/structural/learner.rs) together with existing `M1b`–`M2b` baseline measurements in [`docs/research_record.md`](research_record.md) identifies three structural gaps in the unamended `M2b` wake pruning rules that would otherwise cause Milestone 3 to hit an immediate wall.

### 2.1 Code-Level Diagnosis of the Three Non-Contributing Node Failure Modes

1. **Failure Mode 1 — Single-Node Sensitivity Blindness on Series Involutions/Bijections (`D6`), Cancelling Pairs (`D5`), and Agreement-Gated Clones (`D7b`):**
   - `DiagnosticBuffer::idle_mature_nodes` ([`src/structural/growth.rs:1121`](../src/structural/growth.rs#L1121)) and `EditableCircuit::prune_zero_sensitivity_mature_nodes` ([`src/structural/surgery.rs:302`](../src/structural/surgery.rs#L302)) only test whether **single-node output perturbation** yields zero output change (`output_by_node[v] == 0.0`) or whether removing that set of zero-sensitivity nodes preserves `past_predictions`.
   - **On `D6` (Sensitivity-complete involutions / algebraic identity chains):**
     - At $k = 1$: `Eqz(Eqz(x))` ($\neg(\neg x) \equiv x$).
     - At $k = 2$: `Add(Add(x, ConstLoop(1)), ConstLoop(3))` ($(x + 1) + 3 \equiv x \pmod 4$) or `Sub(ConstLoop(3), Sub(ConstLoop(3), x))` ($3 - (3 - x) \equiv x \pmod 4$).
     - Every node $v$ in `D6` computes a bijection on $x$, so perturbing $v$'s output wire changes `BoundaryOut(p)` on $100\%$ of ticks (`output_by_node[v][p] = 1.0`). Furthermore, every constant or intermediate node emits non-identity values (`1` and `3` for `Add`; alternating values for `Eqz`), so `NodeActivity` recency weights (`nonzero_weight`, `non_one_weight` in [`src/structural/sensitivity.rs:69`](../src/structural/sensitivity.rs#L69)) never decay to zero.
     - Consequently, `port_shares[p][v] / total_ancestry > 0` on every tick: the detour nodes siphon a permanent share of port $p$'s positive prequential reward, never starve in either `Established` or `Juvenile` mode (once mature), and are completely invisible to single-node `D1b.1` zero-sensitivity pruning.
   - **On `D5` (Cancelling pair: `Sub(Add(x, s_0), s_1)` where `(s_0, s_1) = Split(osc)`):**
     - Perturbing `osc` or `Split` perturbs both `s_0` and `s_1` identically, cancelling at `Sub` (`(x + c) - c \equiv x`), so `osc` and `Split` have `output_by_node == 0.0`, whereas `Add` and `Sub` each have `output_by_node == 1.0`.
     - Single-node `D1b.1` therefore places only `{osc, Split}` in `idle_nodes`. Removing `{osc, Split}` in `build_pruned_circuit` ([`src/structural/surgery.rs:625`](../src/structural/surgery.rs#L625)) leaves `Add` and `Sub` with missing inputs (`input_source.is_none()`), cascade-deleting `Add` and `Sub` without bypassing `x` to `BoundaryOut(p)`. The port goes undriven, the `past_predictions` replay check ([`src/structural/surgery.rs:400`](../src/structural/surgery.rs#L400)) fails, and `D1b.1` rejects the prune forever.

2. **Failure Mode 2 — Established-Mode (`debt_paid = true`) Zero-Share Immortality & Unanchored Latch Sensitivity (`D0` stateful, `D1`–`D4` established, `D7a` stateful, `D7b`):**
   - Under the one-time description debt economy ([Charter §3.1.2](foundational_research_charter_v3.md#312-metabolism), DR #25), once a mature node has `debt_paid == true`, `maintenance_costs[v] = 0.0` on all subsequent ticks ([`src/structural/learner.rs:1952`](../src/structural/learner.rs#L1952)).
   - When a zero-share identity arm (`D1` `ConstLoop(0)`, `D2` `onefire` after $\gamma^t$ decay, `D3` `Mul(osc, ConstLoop(0))`, `D4` `ConstLoop(1)`, or `D7b` `Eqz(Sub(a, a'))` feeding `Mul`) is injected in `Established` mode (`debt_paid = true, energy = 4.0`), its per-tick energy change is $\Delta E_v(t) = r_p(t) \cdot (0.0 / Z_p) - 0.0 = 0.0$ on both hits and misses (`E_v(t) = 4.0 > 0.0` forever).
   - In [`src/structural/learner.rs:2097`](../src/structural/learner.rs#L2097), `sensitive[i]` is computed from raw one-tick `output_by_node[i] > 0.0 || latch_by_node[i] > 0.0`:
     - Because perturbing the `0`-arm of `Add` or the `1`-arm of `Mul` alters the operator output, `output_by_node[i]` is `1.0 > 0.0` even though `ring_identity_input_weights_pair` ([`src/structural/sensitivity.rs:794`](../src/structural/sensitivity.rs#L794)) gates its causal weight to `0.0`!
     - Because `latch_by_node[i].iter().any(|&s| s > 0.0)` checks *all* registers in the circuit without verifying backward reachability from `BoundaryOut` or a registered `ComparatorSite::eqz_node`, any disconnected dead branch (`D0` or `D7a`) containing an internal `Delay` register (such as an unconsumed `ConstLoop` or stateful cloned cone `a'`) marks its own dead register latch as sensitive and is never added to `idle_mature_nodes`!

3. **Failure Mode 3 — Destructive "Collapse $\to$ Regrow" in `build_pruned_circuit` (`D2`, `D3`, `D4`, `D7b`):**
   - In [`src/structural/surgery.rs:513-615`](../src/structural/surgery.rs#L513-L615), `EditableCircuit::build_pruned_circuit` currently restricts operator bypass to `inactive_zero_nodes` matching `Primitive::Delay { init: 0 }` with `!activity.ever_nonzero()` (propagated through `Split`).
   - Consequently, when the zero-share arm of **`D2` (`onefire`)** (`Delay { init: 1 }` fed by `ConstLoop(0)`, where `ever_nonzero() == true` even after emitting `0` for `>= W_diag` ticks), **`D3` (`mulzero`)** (`Primitive::Mul` emitting `0` forever), **`D4` (`mulone`)** (`ConstLoop(1)` feeding `Primitive::Mul`), or **`D7b` (`agreement-gated clone`)** (`Eqz(Sub(a, a'))` emitting `1` forever into `Primitive::Mul`) is removed by starvation or idle pruning, `build_pruned_circuit` fails to bypass the splicing `Add`/`Sub`/`Mul` node. Instead, line 625 (`any(|port| input_source(&logical_edges, node, port).is_none())`) **deletes the splicing `Add`/`Mul` node and disconnects `BoundaryOut(p)`**, destroying the working primary predictor and forcing full circuit collapse and regrowth.

### 2.3 Upfront Zero-Free-Parameter Wake Excision Architecture (`M3-E1`, `M3-E2`, `M3-E3`)

To resolve all three failure modes from the start without introducing a single magic number or free threshold, Milestone 3 upgrades Stage-1 wake pruning (`EditableCircuit::prune_zero_sensitivity_mature_nodes` and `build_pruned_circuit` in [`src/structural/surgery.rs`](../src/structural/surgery.rs)) with two exact structural laws and an exogenous injection harness hook:

#### 1. `M3-E1`: Target-Anchored Causal Reachability & General `W_diag` Ring-Identity Operator Bypass

- **Target-anchored frame sensitivity ([`src/structural/learner.rs`](../src/structural/learner.rs), [`src/structural/growth.rs`](../src/structural/growth.rs)):**
  Replace the unanchored `latch_by_node[i].iter().any(|&s| s > 0.0)` check in `ArkheLearner::observe` with target-anchored causal activity:
  1. A node is directly active on tick $t$ iff `output_by_node[i]` is positive on at least one `BoundaryOut` port **or** `comparator_eqz_by_node[i]` is positive on at least one registered `ComparatorSite` **and** its path to that target is not zero-weighted by `ring_identity_input_weights_pair` (`port_shares[p][i] > 0.0` or `comparator_shares[m][i] > 0.0`).
  2. Synchronous register latch sensitivity (`latch_by_node[i][r] > 0.0`) marks node $i$ active **only** if register $r$'s downstream node lies in the backward-reachable cone of at least one `BoundaryOut` port or registered `ComparatorSite::eqz_node` (matching `comparator_and_boundary_active_nodes` in [`src/structural/surgery.rs:478-497`](../src/structural/surgery.rs#L478-L497)). Disconnected dead cycles (`D0` / `D7a` containing internal `Delay` nodes) therefore receive `sensitive = false` and enter `idle_mature_nodes` as soon as they are mature (`juvenile_ticks == 0`) over `W_diag`.
  3. Any mature node $u$ whose output across **all** $W_{\text{diag}} = 64$ buffered frames in `DiagnosticBuffer::node_outputs` is the constant ring-identity element for every downstream consumer edge it drives (`u_out(t) == 0` for all $t \in W_{\text{diag}}$ on `Add`, `Sub.in(1)`, or `Mul`; `u_out(t) == 1` for all $t \in W_{\text{diag}}$ on `Mul`), or whose activity recency weight has decayed below $\gamma^{W_{\text{diag}}}$ (`0.90^{64} \approx 1.18 \times 10^{-3}$) with zero non-identity emissions in`W_diag`, is included in`idle_mature_nodes` together with its exclusive upstream cone.
- **General additive-`0` and multiplicative-`1` bypass in `build_pruned_circuit` ([`src/structural/surgery.rs:500`](../src/structural/surgery.rs#L500)):**
  Generalize `inactive_zero_nodes` in `build_pruned_circuit` (used by **both** `prune_zero_sensitivity_mature_nodes` and `prune_starved_nodes`) to track:
  - `additive_zero_sources`: any removed node $z \in \text{initially\_removed}$ that emitted `0` across all frames of `W_diag` (or has `!activity.ever_nonzero()` / `activity.nonzero_weight <= gamma.powi(W_diag as i32)`), propagated forward through `Primitive::Split` and zero-product `Primitive::Mul` (if either input of `Mul` is in `additive_zero_sources`, `Mul` is added to `additive_zero_sources` and `removed`). When $z \in \text{additive\_zero\_sources}$ feeds `Add(x, z)`, `Add(z, x)`, or `Sub(x, z)` (excluding protected `ComparatorSite::sub_node`), remove the `Add`/`Sub` consumer and rewire survivor `x` directly to all output sinks of that consumer (`bypasses.push((other_source, sink))`).
  - `multiplicative_one_sources`: any removed node $u \in \text{initially\_removed}$ that emitted `1` across all frames of `W_diag` (or has `!activity.ever_non_one()` / `activity.non_one_weight <= gamma.powi(W_diag as i32)`), propagated forward through `Primitive::Split`. When $u \in \text{multiplicative\_one\_sources}$ feeds `Mul(x, u)` or `Mul(u, x)`, remove the `Mul` consumer and rewire survivor `x` directly to all output sinks of `Mul`.
  - **Transitive dead-cone sweep:** After applying additive-`0` and multiplicative-`1` bypasses, iteratively add to `removed` any mature node (not in a protected registered `ComparatorSite`) whose output sinks have all been removed (`output_sinks(&logical_edges, node).is_empty()`), and collapse any `Primitive::Split` left with $\le 1$ surviving output sink.

#### 2. `M3-E2`: Verified Local Pass-Through Excision (`out(v) ≡ x` for $x \in \text{UpstreamCone}(v)$)

When `M3-E1` completes (or finds no constant-identity nodes), Stage 1 checks whether any mature node $v$ in port $p$'s primary cone computes an identity pass-through of an already-existing upstream signal source $x \in \text{UpstreamCone}(v)$ (`SignalSource::BoundaryIn(c)` or `SignalSource::NodeOut { node: u, port }`):

1. **Step 1 — Zero-cost empirical screen over `W_diag` (`64` buffered frames):**
   Using the already-buffered `DiagnosticBuffer::inputs` and `DiagnosticBuffer::node_outputs` (`W_diag = 64` frames), find candidate pairs $(v, x)$ with $x \in \text{UpstreamCone}(v)$ (excluding registered `ComparatorSite` nodes `sub_node` and `eqz_node`) such that $\text{val}(v, t) == \text{val}(x, t)$ for all $t \in \{0, \dots, W_{\text{diag}} - 1\}$ (under `TicksPerHop`, accounting for the hop offset $\Delta h = h(x \to v)$ when $x$ lies on an autonomous cycle of period $P$). Pairs failing on any frame are skipped in $O(1)$.
2. **Step 2 — Exact functional equivalence certification (`Zero-False-Positive Guarantee`):**
   Let $S = \text{ExcisedSubgraph}(v, x) \subseteq V(C)$ be the set of nodes (`v` plus all nodes in $v$'s upstream cone that become unconsumed when $v$'s output edges are rewired to $x$). Before accepting the bypass $v \to x$, prove that $v$ is **formally functionally equivalent** to $x$ across the excised subgraph $S$:
   - **Closed constant-loop invariant detection:** Any 2-node autonomous cycle `Delay { init: c } <-> Split` in or feeding $S$ whose register currently holds $c$ and has no other inputs is certified as the constant source $c \in \mathbb{Z}_{2^k}$.
   - **Combinational pass-through cone ($S \setminus \text{ConstLoops}$ contains zero `Delay` nodes and `wire_delay == Combinational`):** Let $\mathcal{I}_{\text{free}}$ be the distinct external root signal sources entering $S \setminus \text{ConstLoops}$ (tracing through pure `Split` trees so shared `Split` branches `(s_0, s_1)` bind to a single symbol variable $z_s$, and tracing cloned combinational cones in `D7b` back to their common root inputs). Provided $|\mathcal{I}_{\text{free}}| \le \text{EXACT\_SENSITIVITY\_MAX\_BITS}$ (`4`) and $k \le \text{EXACT\_SENSITIVITY\_MAX\_BITS}$ (`4`, so $2^{k |\mathcal{I}_{\text{free}}|} \le 2^{16}$), evaluate $v(\mathbf{z})$ and $x(\mathbf{z})$ exhaustively across **all** $(2^k)^{|\mathcal{I}_{\text{free}}|}$ input assignments $\mathbf{z} \in (\mathbb{Z}_{2^k})^{|\mathcal{I}_{\text{free}}|}$ and require:
     $$\forall \mathbf{z} \in (\mathbb{Z}_{2^k})^{|\mathcal{I}_{\text{free}}|},\quad v(\mathbf{z}) = x(\mathbf{z}).$$
   - **Stateful / `TicksPerHop` pass-through cone ($S$ contains general `Delay` registers or hop shift chains):** Construct the candidate bypassed circuit $C'$ (with autonomous ring register phase shifted by $-\Delta h \pmod P$ when bypassing $\Delta h$ series hops downstream of an autonomous period-$P$ ring in `TicksPerHop`), and require both (a) bounded joint state-transition equivalence from the live register state across all $2^{k K}$ input frames up to `DEFAULT_MAX_CERTIFY_JOINT_STEPS` (when total joint states fit within the exhaustive bound) and (b) $100\%$ bit-identical output predictions on `past_inputs` (`W_diag` frames) and surviving `ComparatorSite::eqz_node` trajectories.
3. **Step 3 — Strict MDL reduction & atomic state-preserving commit:**
   Require $L(C') = \text{circuit\_code\_bits\_with\_tax}(C', 0, \text{split\_code\_tax}) < L(C)$. Commit $C'$ via `recompile_preserving_state`, update debts via `MarginalDebtLedger::on_prune`, reset changed ports, and increment `idle_prune_events` and `local_excision_events`.

#### 3. `M3-E3`: External Detour Injection Hook & Exact Exogenous Conservation Identity

- Add `ArkheLearner::apply_external_edit(&mut self, edited: Circuit, old_to_new: &[Option<NodeId>], injected: &[NodeId], mode: InjectionMode) -> Result<(), GrowthError>` in [`src/structural/learner.rs`](../src/structural/learner.rs):
  - Recompiles `editable` preserving existing register states and node economies via `old_to_new`.
  - In **`InjectionMode::Juvenile`**, calls `self.ledger.on_sprout(&edited, split_code_tax, old_to_new)`, assigns the marginal description debts $\Delta L_v$ (`sum == L(C_post) - L(C_pre)`) to the injected nodes with `debt_paid = false`, sets their `juvenile_ticks` via `compute_debt_amortization_juvenile_ticks` (or base `effective_juvenile_ticks`), and leaves `exogenous_injection_bits` unchanged (`0.0`).
  - In **`InjectionMode::Established`**, updates `self.ledger` so existing nodes keep their debts/paid status and injected nodes have `juvenile_ticks = 0` and `debt_paid = true` (prepaid externally), while incrementing `self.exogenous_injection_bits += L(C_post) - L(C_pre)` so the global epistemic MDL conservation check (`G3.1`) satisfies:
    $$\Delta E(T) \equiv -\Delta L_{\text{null}}^{\text{epistemic}}(T) + \text{exogenous\_injection\_bits}$$
    to machine precision ($\le 10^{-11}$ bits/symbol).

**Key quantitative takeaways for Gate `G3.4` (Rule 9 feasibility):**

1. **Why online primary cones exceed $N_{\min}$ by $0$–$3$ nodes:** Online wake growth stacks additive residual correctors $\hat{y} = y_1 + \hat{r}_1 + \hat{r}_2 \pmod{2^k}$ ([Charter §3.3](foundational_research_charter_v3.md#33-the-first-and-second-neuron-formalised)) without destroying the Epoch-1 scaffold $y_1$. Rewriting a multi-stage stacked corrector into a topologically distinct global minimum (such as replacing a 5-node reactive corrector stack with a 2-node shift chain) requires offline e-graph equality saturation (`M4b`, `H7`).
2. **What `H4` requires in Wake (`M3`):**
   - **Zero post-lock drift:** Once a port locks (`e_curr == 0`), primary-cone node count must never grow monotonically over extended horizons ($N_{\text{primary}}(T = 8000) \le N_{\text{primary}}(T = 2000) \le N_{\text{primary}}(t_{\text{lock}})$, trajectory slope $\le 0.0$ nodes/tick).
   - **Bounded exact-minimum overhead:** On all certifiable tasks ($N_{\min} \le 4, K \le 2$), $N_{\text{primary}}(T = 8000) \le N_{\min} + 4$ (and ratio $N_{\text{primary}} / \max(1, N_{\min}) \le 3.0$).
   - **Full excision of injected bloat:** Any synthetic free-rider detour (`D0`–`D7b`) injected into a locked primary cone is completely excised, restoring $N_{\text{primary}}(T_{\text{end}}) \le N_{\text{primary}}(T_{\text{inj}}^-)$ and $L(C_{\text{primary}}(T_{\text{end}})) \le L(C_{\text{primary}}(T_{\text{inj}}^-))$.
